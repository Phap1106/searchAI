# Landing Page Research Guide

## Scope

Research supporting landing page strategy, conversion copy, headline claims, UX, A/B testing, trust signals, CTA design, and compliance with advertising standards.

## Source Hierarchy

1. Platform primary data: conversion analytics, heatmaps, session recordings, and A/B test results from the specific page or a comparable audience.
2. Peer-reviewed UX and behavioral research: ACM CHI proceedings, Nielsen Norman Group research reports, Journal of Marketing Research.
3. Reputable CRO practitioners with disclosed methodology: CXL Institute, Baymard Institute (checkout and form UX).
4. Platform-published benchmarks (Google, Meta, HubSpot): note industry, funnel stage, and date; do not generalize across contexts.
5. Agency case studies and blog posts: directional only; not standalone evidence for specific claim values.

## Key Questions to Answer

- What is the primary action the visitor must take, and what friction currently prevents it?
- What claims appear on the page, and what evidence supports each?
- Are there regulatory constraints on claims for the jurisdiction and product category (FTC, ASA, or equivalent)?
- What trust signals are present, and are they independently verifiable?
- What does primary data about the target audience reveal about their decision drivers and objections?
- What have controlled tests on comparable pages shown about headline, CTA, and form design?

## Common Evidence Traps

- Conversion rate benchmarks are meaningless without industry, traffic source, device, and funnel stage specified.
- Case study results depend on audience, offer, traffic source, and test rigor; treat as directional only.
- Heuristics ("above the fold," "social proof") are hypotheses, not proven rules; cite the test validating them for a comparable context.
- Superlative claims ("fastest," "best," "#1") require a substantiated basis under applicable advertising standards.

## Output Checklist

- [ ] Every headline or body claim is linked to evidence or flagged as requiring substantiation.
- [ ] Regulatory constraints for the jurisdiction and product category are identified.
- [ ] Benchmark figures include industry, funnel stage, traffic source, and date.
- [ ] Trust signals are assessed for independent verifiability.
